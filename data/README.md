# Inputs, predictions and measurements

The useful chain is a cited physical property, a phase description, a frozen sample prediction and a measured outcome. [Raw records](raw/README.md) and [curated descriptions](curated/README.md) supply the physics. [Held-out conditions](heldout/README.md) preserve what was available before each batch opened; [outcomes](outcomes/README.md) supply the measurements used to test it.

Of the 476 sourced records used by the account, 468 have quotations confirmed at the source and eight have unchecked quotations. Their values and citations stand; the [evidence guide](../docs/data.md#unchecked-quotations) names all eight Fe-Cr-Co inputs and explains the withdrawn checks. [verification.json](verification.json) and [verification_manual.yaml](verification_manual.yaml) preserve the recorded checks. The phase set includes Nd2Fe14B as a rare-earth reference, and some measurement records concern rare-earth-substituted ferrites.
